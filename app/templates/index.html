Frequently Asked Questions

What is Phishinator?
Phishinator is a phishing email analysis tool that examines .eml files and identifies suspicious indicators such as spoofed senders, malicious links, authentication failures, and social engineering techniques.
What file formats are supported?
Currently, Phishinator supports .eml email files. Simply upload an email file and the platform will generate a detailed security analysis report.
Are my uploaded emails stored?
No. Uploaded files are processed only for analysis purposes and are not permanently stored on our servers.
How accurate are the phishing results?
Phishinator uses email header analysis, authentication checks, and phishing indicators to assess risk. While highly effective, results should be used as a security aid and not as the sole basis for critical decisions.
