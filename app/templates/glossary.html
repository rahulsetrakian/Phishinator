Glossary

Here you can find definitions and explanations of common terms related to phishing and email security.

  • Email Authentication

    SPF, DKIM, and DMARC help verify that an email really came from the claimed sender.

  • Email Components

    Header, Reply-To, Return-Path, and MIME define how an email is routed and displayed.

  • Phishing Techniques

    Spoofing, homograph attacks, URL shorteners, QR phishing, smishing, and vishing are common phishing methods.

  • Threat Indicators

    Suspicious domains, urgent language, credential harvesting, and attachment malware are warning signs.

  • Glossary at a Glance

    These terms help explain how phishing emails are built, authenticated, and identified.

  • Sender Identity

    From display names to domain reputation, sender identity shapes how quickly people trust or distrust a message.

  • Security Tools

    Sandboxing, URL scanning, attachment inspection, and email filtering all help catch malicious messages early.

  • Reporting & Response

    Quick reporting, user awareness, and incident follow-up turn phishing signals into faster containment and safer habits.
